When the AI wave hit, I didn't want to just watch—I wanted to roll up my sleeves, write code, build models, and wire up agents myself.

平时我给自己定了个小规矩：只要重复三次的琐碎事，就写个 AI 把它自动化。现在的我，一边维护着台股量化模型和跨境独立站，一边让 Dr eggbot / GrokBot 帮我调度日常工作，周末录录《Tony's Lab》播客聊聊前沿新趋势。把过去几十年的商业直觉，换成今天屏幕上一行行跑得通的逻辑，这种感觉非常有意思。 I live by a simple rule: if a task repeats three times, automate it. Between fine-tuning Taiwan stock quant models, running a cross-border store, having Dr eggbot & GrokBot coordinate daily routines, and recording 'Tony's Lab', turning decades of business instincts into working code has been pure fun.
